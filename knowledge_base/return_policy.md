# Return & Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as they haven't shipped yet. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled - the customer will need to wait for delivery and then request a refund instead.

To return a delivered product, it must be unused and in its original packaging. Pickup is arranged free of charge for damaged or defective items. For all other returns (for example, "no longer needed"), the customer is responsible for shipping the product back.

Return requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery, matching the refund policy's window.

Items like innerwear, perishables, and personalized products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.
