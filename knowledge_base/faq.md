# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I track my order?**
Ask the assistant something like "where is my order ORD101" and it will look up the live status for you. You can find your order ID in your order confirmation email.

**What payment methods are accepted?**
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery for orders under Rs. 5000.

**How do I reach a human agent?**
If the assistant can't fully resolve your issue, it will automatically create a support ticket and a human agent will follow up. You can also ask to "talk to a human" at any point in the conversation.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Right now we only ship within India.

**Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
Address changes are only possible before an order has shipped. Contact support immediately if you need to update it.

**How do I know if my payment went through?**
Your order status page will show "paid" under payment status once the payment is confirmed. If it still shows "pending" after a few minutes, contact support.
