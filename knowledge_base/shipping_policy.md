# Shipping Policy

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days from the order date.

Orders above Rs. 500 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders below that amount have a flat shipping fee of Rs. 49.

An order moves through three stages: "processing" right after it's placed, "shipped" once it leaves the warehouse, and "delivered" once it reaches the customer.

Delivery times may be longer for remote locations or during festival seasons, and customers will be notified by email if there's a delay of more than 2 days beyond the original estimate.

We currently do not offer same-day delivery. Express delivery (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee in select cities.
