# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of the delivery date. The 7-day window starts counting from the day the order is marked "delivered", not the order date.

Refunds are only available for orders that have already been delivered. If an order is still processing or shipped, it should be cancelled instead of refunded.

Refunds for orders above Rs. 5000 require manual approval from a support manager before they are processed. Orders at or below that amount are approved automatically.

Once a refund is approved, the amount is credited back to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

Damaged or defective products are eligible for a full refund regardless of the stated reason, as long as the request is made within the 7-day window. Photo proof may be requested for high-value items.

Refunds are not available for products marked as final sale, or for orders paid through cash on delivery where payment_status is still "pending".
